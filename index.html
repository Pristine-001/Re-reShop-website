What is reShop?

reShop is an app for the Nintendo 3DS / 2DS line of systems that aims to replicate that of the Nintendo eShop which has since been shutdown.

Made for the community, by the community

A small team of two or three is what is behind reShop, we strive to make reShop as good as we can while also preserving licenses!
